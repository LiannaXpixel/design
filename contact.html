Contact ShopLiannaX Support

Contact ShopLiannaX for assistance with digital products, product specifications, file formats, payment confirmation or download access. You can also get in touch about creator collaborations and general store enquiries.

For product support, include the product name and describe the issue you encountered. This helps us understand your question and provide a more useful response.

Use the form below to send your message. Please do not include passwords or full payment-card details.

For questions about an order, mention the product name and the approximate purchase date. For a technical issue, describe what you were trying to do and include any non-sensitive error message shown on screen. This information helps identify whether the question relates to a product, checkout or download access.
